Oppam is a dating app for people aged 18 or older.

## Get help

Email [nihalnclt@gmail.com](mailto:nihalnclt@gmail.com). Tell us what happened
and what you were doing when it happened. We acknowledge messages within 48
hours.

## Your account

**Signing in.** Your account is tied to your phone. You can also link your
Apple or Google account.

**New or lost phone.** If you linked Apple or Google, sign in with the same
account on the new phone. If you did not link either, we may not be able to
recover your account.

**Pause your profile.** You can pause your profile so that new people do not
see you.

## Verification

To get a verified badge, you send a selfie from the app. A person on our side
compares it with your profile photos. The selfie is deleted as soon as the
review is finished, whether it is approved or rejected.

## Safety

**Report or block someone.** Report anyone who makes you uncomfortable, and
block anyone you do not want to hear from. We review every report and may
warn the member, remove their verified badge, remove content, or remove their
account.

**Hide from contacts.** Switch this on to hide your profile from people in
your phone's contact list, and theirs from you. Switching it off deletes the
stored contact fingerprints.

**Distance and last active.** You can switch these off in Safety settings.

**Meeting someone.** Meet in public places, tell someone you trust where you
will be, and never send money to someone you met on the app.

## Plans and payments

Oppam can be used for free. Plus and Premium are paid plans that last a week
or a month and renew until cancelled. Prices are shown in the app before you
buy.

**Cancel.** Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not inside the app.
Cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings, under Subscriptions.

**Refunds.** Apple and Google handle refunds under their own policies. For App
Store purchases, request one at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). For Google Play
purchases, request one from your Google Play account.

## Download your data

Settings, Data and privacy, Download my data gives you a file with your
information.

## Delete your account

Settings, Data and privacy, Delete my account. Your profile, photos, likes and
the text of every message you sent are erased immediately. This cannot be
undone, so download your data first if you want a copy.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Plus or Premium subscription. Cancel
it in the App Store or Google Play first so you are not charged again.
