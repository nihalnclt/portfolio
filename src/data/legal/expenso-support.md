Expenso is a personal expense manager for iPhone. You record what you spend
and earn, and the app shows where your money goes, how your budgets and
savings goals are doing, and what has changed month to month. Everything is
entered by you. Expenso never connects to your bank.

## Get help

Email [nihalnclt@gmail.com](mailto:nihalnclt@gmail.com). Include the email
address of your Expenso account and, if something went wrong, what you were
doing when it happened. We usually reply within two working days.

## Premium subscription

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription bought through the App Store, monthly
or yearly. It removes the limits on goals, accounts, budgets, recurring
payments and custom categories, unlocks every insight and the what-if
planner, lets a budget carry unused money forward, allows typing a transaction
as a sentence, and exports your full history.

**Cancel.** Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. On your
iPhone open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Expenso and tap
Cancel Subscription. You keep Premium until the end of the period you have
paid for.

**Restore.** If you reinstalled the app or signed in on a new phone, open
Settings in Expenso, tap Upgrade to Premium, then Restore purchases. Premium
follows your Apple ID.

**Refunds.** Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Request one at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Export your data

Settings, then Export, shares a CSV file of your transactions that you can
save or send to another app. On the free plan the export covers the most
recent months; Premium exports everything.

## Delete your account

Settings, then Delete account. The app asks for your password, then
permanently removes your account and everything in it: accounts, transactions,
budgets, goals, recurring payments, settings and sessions. This cannot be
undone, so export your transactions first if you want a copy.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in
your iPhone Settings as described above so you are not charged again.
