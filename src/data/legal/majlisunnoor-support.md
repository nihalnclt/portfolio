Majlisunnoor is a devotional reading app for iPhone and Android. It works fully offline.

## Contact

Email [nihalnclt@gmail.com](mailto:nihalnclt@gmail.com) with your question. Include your device model and the app version, and for a display problem, the page where it happens. I usually reply within a few days.

## Common questions

**Do I need an internet connection?** No. All text, page images and fonts are included in the app, so it works without a connection.

**Do I need an account?** No. There is no sign-in and nothing to register.

**Where is my reading position saved?** On your device only. The app remembers where you stopped so you can continue later. It is not synced between devices.

**Can I change how the text looks?** Yes. You can set the text size, line spacing, and a light or dark theme. These settings are also saved on your device.

**How do I reset everything?** Uninstall the app. This deletes your saved reading position and display settings. Reinstall it to start fresh.

**Does the app cost anything?** No. It has no purchases, subscriptions or advertising.

## Privacy

Majlisunnoor collects no personal information. Read the [Majlisunnoor Privacy Policy](/majlisunnoor/privacy) for details.
