Effective date: 18 November 2026

Auto Follow is a Chrome extension made by Nihal N ("we"). This notice explains
what it collects, what stays on your device, and what you can do about it.

Contact: [nihalnclt@gmail.com](mailto:nihalnclt@gmail.com)

## What stays in your browser

The extension keeps its data in your browser's local extension storage. That
includes your settings, the target account you typed, your daily count, a short
activity log of the accounts it followed or unfollowed, and the name, picture
and counts of the profile shown on its dashboard. None of this is sent to us.

The extension works inside your own signed-in X session. It never sees or
stores your X password, and it does not send your X data, your follows or your
activity log to our server.

## What we receive

**If you sign in with Google.** Sign-in is only needed to buy or restore Pro.
Google gives the extension a sign-in token, which our server checks with
Google. From it we read your email address and nothing else. The server then
gives the extension a signed session so it can ask whether your email has Pro.

**If you buy Pro.** Creem, our reseller and Merchant of Record, takes the
payment. We never see your card details. Creem holds your email, billing
country and purchase record. When the extension asks, our server asks Creem
whether your email has a paid, unrefunded Pro purchase.

**Technical data.** Our server and our hosting and network providers keep
short-term logs of requests, including IP address, to run and protect the
service. The checkout and information pages on nihaln.com use privacy-friendly
website analytics that count page views.

We do not keep a customer database of our own. Your Pro status is read from
Creem each time.

## What we do not do

No advertising and no sale of personal data. The extension does not read your
messages, your posts or your browsing on other sites.

## Who processes your data

- **Google**, to confirm who you are when you sign in.
- **Creem**, to take payment, issue receipts and handle tax and refunds.
- **Hosting and network providers**, which run the server and deliver the site.

## How long it is kept

Data in your browser stays until you clear it or remove the extension. Server
logs are kept for a short period. Creem keeps purchase records for as long as
tax and accounting law requires.

## Your choices

- Sign out in the extension's Pro tab to remove the session from your browser.
- Remove the extension to delete everything it stored locally.
- Email us to ask what we hold about you or to ask for it to be deleted. We
  reply within 30 days. Purchase records held by Creem are subject to Creem's
  own retention rules.

## Changes

We may update this notice. The effective date at the top shows the latest
version.
