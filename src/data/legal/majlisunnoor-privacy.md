Effective 3 November 2026

**In short:** Majlisunnoor does not collect, store on any server, or share any
personal information. The app works fully offline. The only things it
remembers, your reading position and your display settings, stay on your
device.

## Who we are

Majlisunnoor is a devotional reading app for iOS and Android, published by
Nihal N ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use
the app.

## Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no forms. It does not ask for
access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or any other
personal information, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

## Information stored on your device

To work as a reader, the app saves a small amount of information in its own
storage on your device:

- **Reading position:** where you stopped reading, so you can continue later.
- **Display settings:** your chosen text size, line spacing, and light or dark
  theme.

The app may also keep temporary cached copies of its own built-in page images
so they display faster. These contain no personal information.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. It is deleted
when you uninstall the app.

## Network use, analytics and advertising

The app does not connect to the internet. All text, page images, and fonts are
included in the app itself. The app contains no analytics, no crash-reporting
services, and no advertising.

## Third parties

We do not sell, share, or transfer any information to third parties, because
we do not collect any. Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) may collect
information when you download the app, under their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from
anyone, it collects no information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled (for
example, by adding advertising), we will update this page and its effective
date before that version is released.
