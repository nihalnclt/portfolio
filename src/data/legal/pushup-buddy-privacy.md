Effective 5 November 2026

Pushup Buddy: Push-Up Trainer is made by Nihal N ("we", "us"). This policy
explains what data the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

**The short version**

- You can use the full training plan without an account. Your data then stays
  on your device and is never sent to us.
- We collect data only if you create an account or subscribe to Premium for
  cloud backup and sync.
- There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell your
  data.

## 1. Without an account

Your training plan, workouts, max tests and settings are stored only on your
device. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Deleting the app
deletes this data.

## 2. With an account

If you sign in, we store:

- **Account details:** your email address, your name if your sign-in provider
  shares it, how you signed in (Apple, Google or email), and the account ID
  Apple or Google gives us for you. If you use Sign in
  with Apple and hide your email, we receive only Apple's relay address.
- **Sign-in sessions:** a hashed sign-in token and a description of the device
  or app version that created it (the "user agent"). These keep you signed in.
- **Email sign-in codes:** the email address the code was sent to and a
  hashed copy of the code. Codes expire after 10 minutes.

## 3. With Premium (cloud backup and sync)

While you have an active subscription, the app uploads your training data so it
can be backed up and synced across your devices:

- workouts (date and time, target and completed reps per set, totals and
  completion, duration, rest time)
- max-test results, including whether each was a personal best
- your training profile (starting, current and goal max, training days per
  week, whether reminders are on, reminder time and rest preference)

We also store your subscription status and its expiry date, which we receive
from RevenueCat.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health records or
advertising identifiers.

## 4. How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, back up and sync your training data, check
whether your subscription is active, and send your sign-in codes. We do not use
your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or rent it.

## 5. Services we rely on

These providers process data only to provide their service to us:

- **Apple and Google:** sign-in, if you choose them. Apple also handles all
  payments; we never see your card details.
- **RevenueCat:** manages subscriptions. It receives your account ID and
  purchase history from the App Store.
- **Resend:** delivers sign-in code emails. It receives your email address and
  the code.
- **Cloudflare:** network protection for our server. It processes your IP
  address when the app contacts our server.
- **Our server:** our database and backups run on a server we operate. Server
  logs record technical request details, such as IP address, time, the
  address requested and the user agent, for security and troubleshooting.

## 6. How long we keep it

- We keep account and training data until you delete your account.
- When you delete your account, your account, training data and sessions are
  deleted from our database immediately.
- Copies may remain in server backups for up to 14 days before they are
  overwritten.

## 7. Deleting your account and your rights

- **Delete your account:** Settings > Account > Delete Account. This
  permanently deletes your account and your cloud data. Workouts saved on your
  device stay until you delete the app.
- **Your subscription is separate:** deleting your account does not cancel it.
  Cancel it in your device's Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.
- **Other requests:** you can also ask us to access, correct, export or delete
  your data by emailing
  [nihalnclt@gmail.com](mailto:nihalnclt@gmail.com). We reply within 30 days.

## 8. Security

Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Sign-in tokens and codes are stored
only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so
there is less to protect.

## 9. Children

Pushup Buddy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us
and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above. If the change
is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
