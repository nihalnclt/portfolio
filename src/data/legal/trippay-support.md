Trippay is a group trip expense splitter for iPhone and Android, listed on the App Store as "Trippay: Split Trip Expenses".

## Contact

Email [nihalnclt@gmail.com](mailto:nihalnclt@gmail.com) with your question. Include the email address on your account and, for purchase issues, the trip name. I usually reply within a few days.

## Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile, then Delete account. It asks for your password to confirm.

Your personal data is erased: your email, password, name, photo and every signed-in session. Trips that only you used are deleted. In trips shared with other people, the expenses you were part of stay, so everyone else's balances remain correct.

## Trip unlock

A trip unlock is a one-time purchase for a single trip. It unlocks multiple currencies, receipt photos, analytics and PDF/CSV export for everyone in that trip, on iPhone and Android. There is no subscription.

**If a purchase did not unlock the trip,** open the trip's Unlock screen and tap "Use a purchase you already made" or "Restore purchases".

**Refunds** are handled by Apple or Google through their normal refund process.
