Effective date: November 3, 2026

Trippay ("the app") helps a group of people record and split the costs of a
trip. It is made by Nihal N ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects,
why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**Account details.** Your email address, a password (stored only as a one-way
hash, never in readable form), your display name, an avatar colour and, if you
add one, a profile photo.

**Trip records you create.** Trip names, destinations and dates; the names of
people in a trip; expenses, advances and payments, with their amounts,
currencies, dates, categories and notes; and receipt or cover photos you
attach.

**Purchases.** When you unlock a trip, Apple or Google processes the payment.
We never see your card or bank details. We receive a record that the purchase
happened (the store, the product and a transaction ID) through RevenueCat, the
service we use to confirm purchases.

**Technical data.** Our server keeps short-term logs of requests, including
your IP address and the address requested, to run and protect the service. We
also store the device name your app reports for each signed-in session, so a
session can be recognised and ended.

## What we do not collect

No advertising, no ad tracking, and no third-party analytics. We do not sell
your data or share it for advertising. The app does not use your location,
contacts or microphone. The camera and photo library are used only when you
choose to attach a photo.

## Who can see your data

- **People in your trips.** Everything recorded in a trip, including your name
  in that trip and expenses you paid or share in, is visible to every member of
  that trip. That is how the group's balances work.
- **Service providers** who process data on our behalf: our hosting provider,
  which runs the server and stores the database and photos; RevenueCat, which
  confirms store purchases (it receives your account ID, not your trip data);
  and Apple or Google, which handle payment.
- **Legal reasons.** We may disclose data if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Server logs are kept only as long
as needed to operate and secure the service.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile, then Delete
account. This permanently removes your email, password, name, photo and every
session. Trips that only you used are deleted.

In trips shared with other people, the expenses and payments you were part of
are kept so that everyone else's balances stay correct. Your name in that trip
stays on those records, but it is no longer linked to any account.

## Children

Trippay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
their data.

## Security

Connections to our server are encrypted, passwords are stored as one-way
hashes, and sign-in tokens expire and rotate. No system is perfectly secure,
but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

## Your choices and rights

You can view and change your name and photo in the app, and delete your
account at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights,
such as to request a copy of your data. Contact us to use them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above, and tell you
in the app if the change is significant.
