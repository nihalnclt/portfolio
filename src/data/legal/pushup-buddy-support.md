Pushup Buddy: Push-Up Trainer is a push-up training app for iPhone.

## Contact

Need help? Email [nihalnclt@gmail.com](mailto:nihalnclt@gmail.com). Include
your device model, iOS version and what happened, and we usually reply within 2
business days.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?** No. The full training plan works offline without one.
You need an account only for Premium cloud backup and sync.

**How do I restore my Premium subscription on a new device?** Sign in with the
same account. Then open the Premium screen and tap Restore Purchases.

**How do I cancel Premium?** On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apple Account >
Subscriptions > Pushup Buddy. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel
it.

**How do I get a refund?** Apple handles refunds. Go to
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**I didn't get my sign-in code.** Check your spam folder and wait a minute.
Codes expire after 10 minutes, and you can request a new one.

**Reminders don't show up.** Check Settings > Notifications > Pushup Buddy on
your iPhone, and the reminder time in the app's Settings.

**Why does Pushup Buddy ask for my calendar?** Only for "Fit it into my day":
it checks when you're busy so your sets land between meetings. Your calendar
never leaves your iPhone. Without access, sets are spread evenly across your
training hours. Change it in Settings > Privacy & Security > Calendars.

**How do I change when my sets happen?** In the app, go to Settings > Training:
pick the hours sets can go in (From and Until), or switch Style to "One session
a day".

**How do I delete my account?** In the app, go to Settings > Account > Delete
Account. This permanently deletes your account and cloud data.
