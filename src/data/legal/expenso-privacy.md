Effective date: 8 November 2026

Expenso ("the app") is a personal expense manager. It is made by Nihal N
("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do
about it.

## What we collect

**Account details.** Your email address, a password (stored only as a one-way
hash, never in readable form) and, if you add one, a display name.

**Preferences.** The currency you choose, the day your financial month starts,
your time zone and locale, your appearance choice, whether app lock is on, and
which reminders you have switched on or off.

**Financial records you enter.** The accounts and wallets you create, with
their names, types and opening balances; the expenses, income and transfers you
record, with their amounts, dates, titles, notes and categories; budgets,
savings goals and contributions; recurring payments and subscriptions; and any
categories you add. Expenso never connects to your bank. Everything in it is
typed in by you.

**Subscription.** If you subscribe to Premium, Apple processes the payment. We
never see your card or bank details. We receive a record that the subscription
exists and when it renews or ends (the store, the product and a transaction
identifier) through RevenueCat, the service we use to confirm purchases.

**Technical data.** Our server keeps short-term logs of requests, including
your IP address and the address requested, to run and protect the service. We
also store the device name your app reports for each signed-in session, so a
session can be recognised and ended.

## What we do not collect

No advertising, no ad tracking, and no third-party analytics or crash
reporting. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. The app does
not use your location, contacts, camera, photos or microphone. If you turn on
app lock, Face ID or Touch ID is checked by your device; we never receive
biometric data.

## How we use it

To show you your own ledger, budgets, goals and reports; to work out insights
such as your biggest category or a budget that is running hot, which are
computed from your own records and nothing else; to keep you signed in; to
confirm your Premium subscription; and to keep the service running and secure.

## Who can see your data

Your records are private to your account. Nobody else using Expenso can see
them.

- **Service providers** who process data on our behalf: our hosting provider,
  which runs the server and stores the database; RevenueCat, which confirms
  store purchases (it receives your account identifier and purchase record,
  not your financial records); Apple, which handles payment; and Cloudflare,
  which provides DNS and HTTPS in front of our server.
- **Legal reasons.** We may disclose data if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Server logs are kept only as long
as needed to operate and secure the service. Database backups are kept for a
short rolling period and then deleted.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings, then Delete
account. The app asks for your password, then permanently removes your
account, settings, accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, recurring payments
and every signed-in session. There is no recovery afterwards, so export your
transactions first (Settings, then Export) if you want a copy.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Subscriptions are
managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then
Subscriptions, and cancel Expenso Premium there.

## Children

Expenso is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
their data.

## Security

Connections to our server are encrypted, passwords are stored as one-way
hashes, and sign-in tokens expire and rotate. No system is perfectly secure,
but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

## Your choices and rights

You can view and change your name and preferences in the app, export your
transactions as a CSV file, and delete your account at any time. Depending on
where you live, you may have further rights, such as to request a copy of
your data or to have it corrected. Contact us to use them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above, and tell you
in the app if the change is significant.
